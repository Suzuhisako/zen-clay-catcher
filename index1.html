How to Play · 遊び方

  • Steering: Slide or drag your finger/mouse across the screen to move the bowl.
  • Shaped (Score): Standard clay (+1), Tenmoku (+2), Kintsugi Gold (+5).
  • Harmony (Streak): Unbroken catches in a row. Missing resets streak to 0.
  • Peak: Your highest harmony streak record saved locally.
